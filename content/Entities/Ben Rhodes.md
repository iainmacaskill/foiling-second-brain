# Ben Rhodes

Ex-Olympic sailor and [[Exmouth]] local; with [[Stevie Morrison]] he partnered in the 49er fleet, winning multiple world championships and representing Great Britain at the 2008 and 2012 Olympics (Ep 65). Now coaches Olympic kite-foil racing and sailing while pursuing his own foiling.
