# AXIS Foils

Foil brand founded by [[Adrian Roper]] — one of the show's most-listened interviews and a recurring reference for foil innovation.

- [[Adrian Roper]] on the new Spitfire range (Ep 36) and, two years later, a mountain of new AXIS innovations (Ep 128 — noted as the most-listened episode they'd done, from Ep 36).
- Ambassador/rider connections: [[Dominic Hoskyns]] is an Axis ambassador (Ep 44); [[Frank|Franks Foilboards]] shown in full red Axis livery at AWSI (Ep 91).

## Ripple additions (batch 2, 2026-07-20)

- Tiesda You (Starboard, Ep 110) characterises Axis's system as a saddle front/tail-wing connection with a socket mast mount; says Axis's wide saddle 'flanges' became a constraint as wings got thinner, prompting their Black Series fuselage.
- Klaas Voget (Duotone, Ep 73) names Axis (with Armstrong) as Duotone's main foil competitor.
- Julien Salles (F-One, Ep 47) contrasts F-One's foil philosophy with AXIS's and Armstrong's move to high-camber downwind foils, a direction F-One tried and abandoned.
