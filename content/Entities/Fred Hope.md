# Fred Hope

Kitefoiler and [[Slingshot]] rider whose kitefoil riding is credited on the show with "changing the game" — instantly recognisable on the water.

- First pinned down at Slingshot's [[Hood River]] offices (Ep 84).
- Returns to discuss Slingshot's "Project Drag" board programme, the shape underpinning Liam's home-made board (Ep 144).

What Ep 144 adds:
- Based in [[Hood River]] / the Gorge; his role at [[Slingshot]] is essentially product storytelling — he drove **Project Drag**, bringing [[Ken Adgate]]'s Maui board and a Tuttle-mast setup to production with [[Kyle Touhey]] and an in-house box-builder ("Michael"). Girlfriend is a wealth-management advisor.
- Champions **radical transparency** — set up the Project Drag **Discord** for direct designer–consumer contact and posts factory photos he'd previously never have shared.
- Fluent on the **mast thinness-vs-stiffness** trade-off and Tuttle geometry (15.95mm head); careful about managing Ken/Kyle/Michael as *friends*, not employees. Lost £170 to an AI-picked investment (a running gag into Ep 147).

What Ep 84 adds (the earlier, first sit-down — recorded live at [[AWSI]] in [[Hood River]]):
- Nearly left foiling altogether: he called Slingshot a few months before graduating (around what he calls his career peak, December 2021) to say he was quitting to become a marine biologist, but stayed on. At Slingshot his role is closer to product owner — he writes product briefs, works with R&D and designers, then writes marketing copy and works with sales through to launch.
- Drove the origin of Slingshot's screwless **OneLock** foil connection roughly three years before this recording, starting from two practical problems — corrosion on the old Phantasm aluminium fuselage and fiddly beach setup for less dexterous customers — that only later turned into a drag-reduction project once the team realised how much drag the screws themselves added. Broke roughly 50 masts during development shrinking the carbon collar, forcing repeated (expensive) mold retools.
- Expects track systems (Slingshot's current RTS/Rapid Track) to fade within about three years, but argues no single brand can move to a shared connection standard alone — cites [[Connor Bainbridge]]/"Ray Sir"/Isaac's UK adapter board (Chewbacca/Levitas) as evidence of real demand, and floats a Tuttle-style shared slide-with-pucks idea.
- Close friend of [[Ken Adgate]] — rides Ken's boards for fun despite them being narrower/more advanced than Slingshot's general customer needs; echoes Ken's view that boards need tail flex to allow pumping, backed by his own back-to-back downwind board test.
- Runs kite-foiling coaching: a clinic in Cabarete with guide "Andreas" ("Uncharted Kite Sessions") and private coaching for ~5 clients worldwide, capped at ~6 weeks a year. Thinks kite foiling is shrinking as a discipline but was the catalyst that let foiling expand into wing/downwind/SUP. Personal kitefoil setup: 92cm mast, 750 front wing with a deliberately inefficient/low-glide profile. Most proud of the OneLock foil and the minimalist Hobie/Hopecraft Joystick and UFO kite-foiling gear.
