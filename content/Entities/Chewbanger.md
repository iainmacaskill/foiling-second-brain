# Chewbanger

Formula Kite racing foil. [[Daniela Moroz]] (Ep 88) switched to Chewbanger just five months before the 2024 Olympics, having never won a Worlds title on it before — it went on to win gold in both fleets at the Games. Also referenced in Ep 144 (Fred Hope/Slingshot Project Drag) as a rival connection standard.
