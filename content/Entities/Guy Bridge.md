# Guy Bridge

One of the UK's most rounded watermen — expert across nearly all foiling disciplines — and, with partner [[Emma Clamp]], dubbed the "King and Queen of UK foiling" by the hosts.

- The podcast's very first guest, walking through how he picked up each discipline and his favourite gear (Ep 5).
- Post-wedding/honeymoon catch-up with Emma (Ep 104); the couple's wedding debriefed (Ep 109).
- Ep 145, "Guy's Big Send" — his downwind run across the whole UK south coast, told with Emma a week later. A Guy Bridge downwind video is flagged earlier (Ep 61).
- Repeatedly named across the pilot episodes as the hosts' **local [[Exmouth]] inspiration for getting into winging** — both cite watching him rip (dropping the wing to pump around on waves) as the moment they ordered wing gear (Ep 1, Ep 3). Prones on a small Armstrong 690MA (Ep 147).
