# Robby Naish

Co-founder of [[NAISH]] (referred to as "Robbie" in Ep 123, tentative identification). Sold the company ~2.5 years before Ep 123's recording to NAISH's long-time European distributor. Praised by Mike Raper (Ep 24) as one of the last genuinely rider-founded brand owners.
