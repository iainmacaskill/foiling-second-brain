# Luke Atkinson

Big Aussie [[Prone Foiling|prone]] foiler — billed as Australia's and France's best prone foiler of 2025 (and "UK's second best").

- First appears hanging out in the US with [[Ken Adgate]] (Ep 81).
- Solo feature on surf foiling, kit, riding style, judging and his habit of "ballsing up" downwind races (Ep 131). His enthusiasm for a [[Frank|Franks Foilboards]] board is noted in Ep 91.
