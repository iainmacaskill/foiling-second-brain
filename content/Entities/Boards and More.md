# Boards and More

Umbrella company for [[Fanatic]], [[Duotone]], IOM and SQlab. Traces back to old windsurf brands F2/Mistral/Fanatic and a licensed North/North Kiteboarding sail brand (Ep 73, Ep 24). Licensed the North Sails brand for kiteboarding from ~2001; when that licence ended ~2018 launched Duotone itself, while [[North]] was rebuilt separately by Mike Raper.
