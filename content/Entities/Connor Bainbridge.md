# Connor Bainbridge

British 2024 Formula Kite Olympian, co-founder of Blue Fox Boards and an [[Ozone]] designer (Ep 126). The episode covers Paris 2024, the operations behind Blue Fox Boards, and the value of giving designers critical feedback.
