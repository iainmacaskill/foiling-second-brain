# Lift Foils

One of foiling's original brands ("big dogs / real OGs"), covered through founder [[Nick Leason]] (Ep 49), who tells the story of the sport's early product development. [[Kahi]] rides Lift and is a fan-favourite of Liam's (Ep 48). Lift foils/testing also comes up in a gear-catch-up (Ep 71).

## Ripple additions (batch 2, 2026-07-20)

- Ep 38 (Armie Armstrong): names the Lift HAX 110 as one of five non-Armstrong foils he'd want to try, praising its glide-for-size.
- Ep 47 (Julien Salles, F-One): names Lift as the non-F-One brand he respects most, for early leadership on extreme aspect ratio and monoblock fuselage design.
