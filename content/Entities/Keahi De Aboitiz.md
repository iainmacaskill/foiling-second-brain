# Keahi De Aboitiz

4x world champion wave kiter and top shredder (Ep 6) — Freddie's first solo interview, set up by Karl Cadwallader of [[Cabrinha]] UK at the Hayling Island BKSA Championship.

- **2026-07-20 (Ep 6 detail):** Australian-born, now largely Hawaii-based (mother is Hawaiian, giving him a US passport); moved to Hawaii close to full-time once COVID made the regular commute pointless. First tried kite foiling almost by accident on a no-wind day in Maui around 2012 (the year of his first world title), crediting the wider explosion of [[Kite Foiling]] to "Go Foil" bringing out the first thicker, small-wave-friendly foil (no existing page for the brand). Sponsored internationally by [[Cabrinha]] since roughly 2011, with a brief spell on Neil Pride foil gear before returning to Cabrinha for everything foil-related. Rides Cabrinha's H-series foils (mainly the 800, plus 650/1000/1200 and an unreleased ~700 sample) and X-series wings, and a smaller "Links" board range (usual go-to a 4'5"/44L at 80kg). Prefers surfing when it's pumping, kiting when windy with waves, and wing foiling in lighter/lull conditions — describing wing foiling as closest to tow foiling. Relatively new to downwind foiling but growing into it, rating [[Hood River]] as ideal because an outgoing tide against the wind stacks the swell.
