# Fanatic

Historic windsurf-board brand under [[Boards and More]], sister brand to [[Duotone]]. [[Klaas Voget]] rode for Fanatic from 1992; Fanatic originally owned foiling/wing-boards before a 2024-era merger into Duotone's wing-foiling division, triggered when a new front wing designed for wingers was also wanted by kitefoilers (Ep 73). Sub-foil board shaper Sky Solbach pulled Klaas into surf foiling and co-designed the first Fanatic "Skywing" wingboard.
