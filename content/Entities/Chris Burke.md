# Chris Burke

Runs Poseidon Watersports (a kite/wing school in Poole, plus a Newquay shop venture) and organises the Poseidon Kernow Foil Classic in [[Cornwall]] (Ep 30). A good friend of the hosts and a "Wang Lord".

- Poseidon Watersports and his industry grasp (Ep 30); the inside scoop on the Kernow Foil Classic surf comp, becoming a "Wang Lord" (Ep 45).
- Named early as an advocate of smaller boards who talks the hosts "into smaller boards again," and Freddie's host for his favourite early winging session — riding clean swell at **Avon Beach**, Bournemouth (Ep 3). By 2026 he's weighing up whether to offer parawing lessons/coaching (Ep 147).
