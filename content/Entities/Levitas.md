# Levitas

Formula Kite racing foil brand. One of the dominant race foils alongside [[Chewbanger]]; [[Daniela Moroz]] (Ep 88) rode Levitas after leaving [[Mikes Lab]] (which declined to register for Olympic racing) before a late switch to Chewbanger ahead of the 2024 Games. Also referenced in Ep 144 (Fred Hope/Slingshot Project Drag).
