# Cynthia Brown

Cynthia "Cynbad" Brown, "Ambassador of Adrenaline" (Ep 83) — a legend of the sports, met while she worked the [[Ozone]] stand at the AWSI show. From competing in windsurfing in the 1980s to pioneering "hobby parachute" downwinding (an early [[Parawing]]-style approach) now.
