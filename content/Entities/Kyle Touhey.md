Board-shape collaborator who, with [[Ken Adgate]], provided the shape for Liam's DIY board project and sits down to review it (Ep 138) — the same episode that reveals a big brand was chasing a Ken Adgate board.

- Ep 144: handles the **Shape3D CAD** side of [[Slingshot]]'s **Project Drag** with Ken Adgate; the biggest Project Drag size is dubbed "the Kyle board." He shapes plenty he never films, so the range tends to get attributed to Ken.
