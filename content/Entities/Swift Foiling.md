# Swift Foiling

UK maker of foil boards, including "Javelin"-style [[Downwind Boards]], run by Dom and Angus (Ep 11). One of the show's early looks at UK downwind board building. *Note: this Dom is distinct from [[Dominic Hoskyns]] of [[FoilDrive]].*
