# Jorrit Buurma

The "lesser-known Buurma brother" — co-runs and operates [[Appletree]] with [[Wieger Buurma]], based in Portugal running the factory and surfing daily (Ep 135). The hosts wanted him in the limelight since meeting him in 2024.

- First full interview (Ep 135): self-describes as technical designer / R&D / production manager, having recently handed the production-manager title to a new hire ("Mark") to free up time for R&D. Was one of the two co-founders (with [[Wieger Buurma]]) who moved Appletree's production to Portugal, choosing it over Eastern Europe for the surf and warmth; deliberately avoids the brand/business side of interviews and prefers talking to riders and OEM partners.
- Currently leads Appletree's hollow-board construction project personally, aimed at cutting swing weight and matching competitor foam weights in 80L+ boards without giving up durability (Ep 135).
- Personal riding: estimates more than half his own sessions are on [[FoilDrive|Foil Drive]] (both Max and Slim batteries, not Fusion), with the rest split between proning (his preference) and wing foiling (Ep 135).
