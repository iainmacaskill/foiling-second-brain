# Keith Teboul

Head shaper behind [[KT Foiling]] (KT Surfing / KT Foiling / Quatro Maui / Goya Windsurfing / MFC Hawaii) and an all-round waterman (Ep 42). Covers his story, the origins of the brands, the aftermath of the Maui wildfires, the state of the leisure-goods industry, and windsurf-vs-foil board construction.
