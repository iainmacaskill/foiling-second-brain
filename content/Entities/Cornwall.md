# Cornwall

Cornwall (Kernow) — the South West surf-coast region that hosts the Kernow Foil Classic and much of the hosts' [[Prone Foiling|prone]] progression, centred on Newquay.

- The Kernow Foil Classic, run by [[Chris Burke]]/Poseidon Watersports, is treated as the UK event of the year (Ep 45, Ep 46, Ep 124), supported by the podcast and [[Kitebarn]].
- A breakthrough prone run in Newquay (Ep 70); the hosts hang with the [[CODE Foils]] boys in Cornwall (Ep 106).
