# Steph Bridge

Of Edge Watersports in [[Exmouth]], with husband [[Eric Bridge]] (Ep 69). From starting a small sailing school in the 1980s to running one of the UK's most successful kite/wing schools and shops — highly regarded in the wing/kite industry.

- Effectively [[Liam]]'s **boss** — she sent him out on his very first winging session (autumn 2020) on an [[F-One]] Swing, "wind-with-tide," and encouraged him to try demo foils; he fell for the [[F-One]] Phantom 1080 through her setup (Ep 3).
