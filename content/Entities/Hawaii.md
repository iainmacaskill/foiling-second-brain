# Hawaii

Hawaii — the spiritual home of much of foiling on the show, and the reference point for elite [[Downwind]] racing (the Molokai 2 Oahu / M2O and the Hawaiian race series).

- M2O results discussed via [[Oskar Johansson]]'s 2nd place (Ep 40); Hawaiian races followed at a distance (Ep 112); [[Kane De Wilde]] on the Hawaii downwind race series and racing mindset (Ep 136).
- Hawaii-based guests/brands: [[KT Foiling]]/[[Keith Teboul]] (Ep 42), [[Cynthia Brown]] (Ep 83), and [[Kahi]]'s work with Sustainable Coastlines Hawaii (Ep 48).
