# Rich Boughton

"Big Richy B", the "Godfather of UK Winging" — runs [[Kitebarn]] (kite/wing retail and repair) and is the show's original inspiration: the podcast idea formed on drives to see him at his home spot of [[Bantham]] (Ep 12).

- A founding "Wang Lord": returns for the WangLordz threeways (Ep 16, Ep 39) and the recurring Christmas hot-tub specials (Ep 52, Ep 94, Ep 134).
- Ep 3 sets him up early: the hosts ride with him at [[Bantham]] and call him "the endless summer of wing foiling" — a drawn-out, controlled-carve style (vs Liam's critical short-board style), and a serial gear-reviewer for the foiling magazines. He's the **technical editor of Foiling mag**; his wife **Sarah** edits Kite Mag — she's the route by which Freddie got the Mauritius media gig (Ep 147).
