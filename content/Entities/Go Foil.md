# Go Foil

Hawaii-based foil brand. Credited by Keahi De Aboitiz (Ep 6) as sparking the surf/kite-foil boom with the first thicker, less-efficient foil built for riding small waves rather than pure performance — no UK distributor, per Keahi. Dave Kalama (Ep 142) rode Go Foil for most of his career before switching to [[F4]] in the last year.
