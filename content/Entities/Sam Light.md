# Sam Light

OG wakestyle kiter turned [[Slingshot]] Sports global marketing/social (and ex-Team Manager), avid wing and kitefoiler and YouTuber (Ep 41). One of the highest-level riders the UK has produced. The hosts later got Slingshot foils off Sam in the UK (Ep 127).
