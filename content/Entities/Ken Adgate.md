# Ken Adgate

A man with "fingers in many pies" — shredder, board builder, un/official test rider for [[Ozone]] and [[Mikes Lab]], and a US car-dealership owner. One of the show's most-quoted friends after he became a listener and joined in.

- Ep 60, "King of the Tacks" — his solo debut, and background: based in Rio Vista, Northern California (the Sacramento Delta), riding evenings at Sherman Island; day job running family car dealerships. Came to kiteboarding once his kids outgrew the family boat, hooked instantly after watching [[Johnny Heineken]] and "Ruben" videos. Became [[Mikes Lab]] founder Mike (Zajcik)'s regular on-water test partner and best friend, describing himself as "Robin and Johnny's Batman" — Johnny Heineken's sidekick tester; also an un/official [[Ozone]] test rider, feeding back on prototypes as "part of the program." A hobbyist board builder (~120 boards for friends by this point) with a detailed, foil-first design philosophy: shape the board to the foil's angle of attack rather than the reverse, favouring narrow (~18.75–19in) rockered boards, half-inch concave decks, chines, and V-tail/V-nose shaping for cleaner touchdown deflection. His signature "tack" — throwing the wing across the shoulder to pivot into a fast, banked turn, learned from strapless pocket-board kite freeriding — gives the episode its title.
- Solo feature: "King of the Tacks" (Ep 60); voice-note contributions on flat-water wing tricks (Ep 67) and board-building thoughts (Ep 64).
- In the US: with [[Luke Atkinson]] in Kenny's garage (Ep 81) and introducing the hosts to [[Johnny Heineken]] (Ep 85).
- His board shape underpins Liam's DIY builds, reviewed with [[Kyle Touhey]] (Ep 138).
- Ep 144: the driving force behind [[Slingshot]]'s **Project Drag** — he builds ~50 boards a year for friends "not for the money," and offered his Maui board/Tuttle learnings to Slingshot to get more people on the product. His board sharing on Instagram (progression from a race-kite board with a foil bolted on) is credited for the demand; asked only that a board be donated to a cancer raffle in return.
