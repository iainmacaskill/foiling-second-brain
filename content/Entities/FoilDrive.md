# FoilDrive

Maker of the motor-assist drive that defines the show's [[Foil Assist]] discussions — a brand the hosts return to often and clearly rate.

- Co-founder [[Paul Martin]] is one of the most popular guests (Ep 80; the FUSION in Ep 130).
- [[Dominic Hoskyns]] leads FoilDrive in Europe (Ep 44, Ep 55); [[Paul Cooper]] rides it for surf-foil airs (Ep 120); [[Josh Ku]] is a worldwide FoilDrive ambassador (Ep 89).
- A Code/FoilDrive integrated mast is discussed with [[CODE Foils]] (Ep 118).
