# Martin Fisher

Designer/technologist credited by [[Tiesda You]] (Ep 110) with running [[Starboard]] Foils' computer optimisation process for front wings — Tiesda says the optimised output beat anything he could hand-shape, becoming the basis of Starboard's "7000X" (~7000 iteration loops) marketing.
