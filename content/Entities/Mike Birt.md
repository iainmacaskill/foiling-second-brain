# Mike Birt

OG of the UK windsports scene — an ex-pro windsurfer and the very first British Kitesurf Champion, and now UK distributor for [[Airush]], AK, Shinn ([[Mark Shinn]]) and Freewing (Ep 51). Opened the show's more in-depth look at the UK windsports industry; connected the hosts to [[Clinton Filen]] (Ep 54).
