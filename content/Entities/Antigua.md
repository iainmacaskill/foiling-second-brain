# Antigua

Antigua — the Caribbean kite/wing spot Liam visited over successive winters, a recurring backdrop for early episodes recorded "at opposite ends of the world".

- Liam's Antigua trips frame Ep 13 (mast stiffness, recorded across hemispheres) and Ep 14 (return to a home-spot session); [[Jake Kelsick]] runs a kite/wing school in Antigua (Ep 15).
