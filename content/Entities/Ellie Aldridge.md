# Ellie Aldridge

UK kite racer who qualified to represent the UK at the July 2024 Olympics (Ep 56). Runs through the whole Olympic Formula Kite process and her path from 49erFX sailing to [[Kite Foiling]]. Revisited briefly in the Team GB Olympics catch-up (Ep 76).

## Ripple additions (batch 2, 2026-07-20)

- Won gold in the 2024 Olympic Formula Kite women's fleet, Marseille (per Ep 88, Daniela Moroz).
