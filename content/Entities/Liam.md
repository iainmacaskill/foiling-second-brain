# Liam

Co-host of [[The Generic Foiling Podcast]] alongside [[Freddie]]. Foiler with a watersports-industry background; the show follows him and Freddie dissecting their own riding across foiling disciplines. His winging backstory is covered in Ep 3.

What the catalogue reveals:
- Based in Devon around [[Exmouth]]; a keen tinkerer and DIY board-builder, building boards off [[Ken Adgate]]/[[Kyle Touhey]] shapes (Ep 138) and later a [[Slingshot]] "Project Drag" setup (Ep 144). Bought and renovated a house through 2026 (Ep 140, Ep 147).
- The more technically-minded host — repeatedly drills designers on engineering (e.g. [[Charles Bertrand]] on [[Masts]], Ep 26/32).
- Media work: reviewed kites at the 2023 North Dealer Meeting in Portugal for [[Rou Chater]]'s IKSURF/Tonic team (Ep 37, Ep 86).
- Winter [[Antigua]] trips (Ep 13, Ep 14); both US [[Hood River]] trips (2024, 2025). A founding "Wang Lord" with [[Freddie]], [[Rich Boughton]] and [[Chris Burke]].

From the pilot episodes and recent catch-ups:
- Works the **teaching/retail** side of watersports at a shop on the Exmouth seafront; an engineer by trade and coaching-minded — he does a "pre-session assessment" of conditions vs objectives and preaches "count the victories" (Ep 1). Became a qualified instructor around 2021 (Ep 1). His mum **Georgie** gave feedback on the pilot (Ep 1).
- Got into **kitefoiling early**, before off-the-shelf kitefoil gear existed, through his work colleagues; "missed the introduction" of foiling during a break from kiting, and "made the wrong purchase" first time (Ep 1).
- **Winging start:** first session autumn 2020, sent out by his boss [[Steph Bridge]] on an [[F-One]] Swing 5m; it "clicked" on session two behind the Exmouth sandbar. First own kit was an RRD wing + RRD foil on a converted formula-kite raceboard; fell for the [[F-One]] Phantom 1080. **Built his own board** (~90L, 5'2", a full-carbon Ride Engine Moon Buddy copy, drilled to save weight). Rides **front-strap-only** after an ankle injury — but plans to go strapless more in 2026 (Ep 3, Ep 147).
- Rode the [[Slingshot]] "Project Drag" board in **track** form for six months and did back-to-back mast tests ([[F-One]] 12 vs 14mm; Slingshot plate vs Tuttle), concluding the standout gain is **touchdown recovery**, not raw speed (Ep 144).
- 2026: buried in a **house renovation** (rewiring, kitchen re-floor/insulation, plumbing); partner **Eleanor** does the decorating. This plus no wind kept the show quiet for ~two months (Ep 147).
