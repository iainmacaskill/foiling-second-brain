# Exmouth

Exmouth, Devon — the hosts' home town and home spot, and the base for many of their "musings" catch-up episodes. Home of Edge Watersports.

- Home-spot sessions and gear reflections: low-volume wing boards ridden here (Ep 14), "Exmouth Musings" catch-ups (Ep 112, Ep 115).
- Local figures: [[Steph Bridge]] & [[Eric Bridge]] of Edge Watersports (Ep 69); Olympian coaches [[Ben Rhodes]] & [[Stevie Morrison]] are Exmouth locals (Ep 65); [[Guy Bridge]] the local winging inspiration (Ep 1, Ep 3).
- Both hosts learned to wing here around 2020; the spot has strong tidal currents and a sandbar "speed strip" of flat water, and produces good wind-swell in a south-easterly — variable, often sub-par conditions that shaped Liam's expectation-management mindset (Ep 3). By 2026 the show's "musings" are often recorded here (Liam's garden in the Ep 147 heatwave).
