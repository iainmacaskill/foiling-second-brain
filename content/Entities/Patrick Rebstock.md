# Patrick Rebstock

Old-school strapless kitesurf pioneer turned [[Downwind]] and [[Parawing]] "frother" (Ep 113). The hosts consumed his early strapless riding while learning to kite; he now posts downwind runs along the Oregon and Californian coast, and hosted them on their US road trip.
