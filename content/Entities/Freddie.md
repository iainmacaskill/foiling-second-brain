# Freddie

Co-host of [[The Generic Foiling Podcast]] alongside [[Liam]]. Foiler with a watersports-industry background; competes/travels for events. His winging backstory is covered in Ep 3.

What the catalogue reveals:
- Based in Devon around [[Exmouth]]; the less tech-obsessed host, often "bringing it back in line for the masses" when Liam and a guest go deep on foil design (e.g. Ep 62).
- Travels widely for the sport: a [[Mauritius]] kitesurf event (Ep 147) and a New Zealand "Kiwi experience" across disciplines (Ep 137).
- Big on [[Prone Foiling]] and, later, [[Foil Assist]] and [[Parawing]]/[[Downwind]] paddle-ups. Both US [[Hood River]] trips (2024, 2025). A founding "Wang Lord" with [[Liam]], [[Rich Boughton]] and [[Chris Burke]].

From the pilot episodes and recent catch-ups:
- Works the **gear-distribution / sales-rep** side of the industry — distributes [[Ozone]] kites in the UK and has repped [[AXIS Foils|Axis]] foils. His motivation swings hard with how the last session went (Ep 1). ~82kg (Ep 3).
- **Kite pedigree:** kitesurfed 15+ years and kite-buggied/landboarded before that — a **Flexifoil team rider** (in their ~2008/09 catalogue), the brand that also sponsored 5× champion Aaron Hadlow (Ep 1, Ep 144).
- **Winging start:** learned around late 2020 (first glide at an Andy Gratwick demo/instructor course); ~82kg on an SIC 115L board, big Axis beginner wing and an [[Ozone]] Wasp 5m; admits he "dropped down too quickly" and under-practised because he'd choose kiting when it got windy. Now on the Axis ART 999 (Ep 3).
- Learned to foil **behind boats** in 2022 (dad's 19ft ski boat, a friend's 28ft Axopar "HMS Basher") — running the [[F-One]] Phantom 1480/1080 (Ep 3).
- 2026: nearly all his **turnover is now [[Parawing|parawing]]** (Ozone Pocket Rocket V2 outselling V1) while **wing sales have "dropped off a cliff"**; went to Mauritius as *media* for a flat-water kite event and — to his embarrassment — never rode a wave there (Ep 147).
