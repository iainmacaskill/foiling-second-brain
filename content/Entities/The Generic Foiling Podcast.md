# The Generic Foiling Podcast

Foiling / watersports podcast hosted by [[Liam]] and [[Freddie]], running since 31 July 2022. 147 episodes as of 17 July 2026 (Ep 147, "Casa Del Liam and Freddies trip to Mauritius"), averaging ~89 minutes. The hosts dissect their own riding as they move through new foil-based disciplines and bring in guests — riders, shapers and industry figures — from across the foiling world.
