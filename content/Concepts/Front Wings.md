# Front Wings

The main lifting foil under the fuselage — the component that most defines a foil's feel. A perennial tech topic on the show, especially the "high aspect vs mid aspect" question (Ep 7, "High Aspect Hype").

- Aspect-ratio debate: are high-aspect wings all they're cracked up to be? (Ep 7).
- Designer-level detail from [[Charles Bertrand]] of [[F-One]] (Ep 26), [[Rob Whittall]] of [[Ozone]] (Ep 34, Ep 35), and [[Adrian Roper]] of [[AXIS Foils]] on the Spitfire range (Ep 36).
- New-generation foils chasing combined glide, speed range and [[Pumping|pumpability]] (Ep 52); [[F-One]]'s Plume and Frigate as new-discipline shapes (Ep 111).

## Ripple additions (batch 2, 2026-07-20)

- Ep 110 (Tiesda You, Starboard): deep dive on connection-system history (saddle vs sleeve vs socket) and UCS's saddle-plus-sleeve hybrid design.
- Ep 47 (Julien Salles, F-One): Skate (F-One's best-seller ever), Seven Seas (underrated downwind glide) vs Phantom (beginner/intermediate) positioning; confirmed Phantom V2 in development.
- Ep 73 (Klaas Voget, Duotone): Whiz front wing designed with wingtips turned up (not down) for loose roll, easy pump, low stall speed for wave/prone riding — superficially resembles the F-One Spitfire outline but rides differently.
- Ep 123 (Ewan Jaspan, NAISH): square/thicker foil tips breach more progressively and predictably, producing dramatic spray, vs pointy tips.
