# Foil Assist

Motor-assisted foiling — a small electric drive fitted to the board that helps a rider get up on foil and catch waves, most associated on the show with [[FoilDrive]]. Also encompasses dockstarting technique.

- [[Paul Martin]], FoilDrive co-founder, is a repeat guest (Ep 80, Ep 130 on the FUSION) and one of the show's most popular interviewees.
- [[Dominic Hoskyns]] runs FoilDrive's European growth (Ep 44, Ep 55); [[Paul Cooper]] is "THE foil-assist athlete", using it to boost surf-foil airs (Ep 120).
- [[CODE Foils]] discuss a Code/FoilDrive integrated mast (Ep 118).
