# Downwind Boards

Long, narrow, high-volume paddle boards built to get a rider up onto swell for [[Downwind]] foiling — a distinct board category the show tracks closely as downwinding grows.

- "Javelin"-style downwind boards from [[Swift Foiling]] (Ep 11); airline/board-length constraints joked about (Ep 100).
- The hosts borrow downwind boards at [[Hood River]] (Ep 103, from [[Aidan Nicholas]]).
- Design and paddle-up philosophy from [[Kane De Wilde]] on hollow-board construction (Ep 136) and the [[Voyager Foiling]] crew (Ep 96).
- Best-foilboard picks for downwind SUP (Ep 106).

## Ripple additions (batch 2, 2026-07-20)

- Ep 24 (Mike Raper, North): confirms North has a downwind board-and-foil programme in active development.
