# Downwind

Foiling downwind by catching open-ocean or coastal swell, usually paddling up on a long, narrow [[Downwind Boards|downwind board]] (SUP paddle) or increasingly with a [[Parawing]]. A dominant through-line of the catalogue's later years, from the hosts declaring themselves "downwinderers now" (Ep 74) onward.

- Deep-dive coaching from [[James Casey]] on how to downwind (Ep 17) and from the [[Voyager Foiling]] crew via [[Simeon Ke-Paloma]] (Ep 96).
- The [[Hawaii|Molokai 2 Oahu]] race and Hawaiian race series recur as reference points (Ep 40, Ep 112, Ep 136).
- World-record and big-send stories: [[Olivia Piana]]'s downwind record (Ep 58) and [[Guy Bridge]]'s south-coast "Big Send" (Ep 145).
- The [[Parawing]] vs downwind-SUP debate is a running argument (Ep 108, Ep 109).
- By 2026 the hosts reckon [[Parawing|parawing]] has **"murdered" downwind paddle foiling for the masses** — cheap carbon downwind boards (~€600, near cost) sit unsold, though the committed few still do it (Ep 147).
- **Racing as the sport's "F1"** (Ep 147): the season's first Hawaiian downwind race was won by [[Edo Tanas]] (Kai second) — riding **[[F4]] foils**, not sponsor [[Slingshot]]'s (who lacked a downwind foil in time). F4 dominated the field the way [[AXIS Foils|Axis]] had the year before; [[Armstrong]] and [[F-One]] are seen as "backing away" from the race/downwind niche.

## Ripple additions (batch 2, 2026-07-20)

- Keahi De Aboitiz (Ep 6), newer to downwind himself, describes bump-linking as almost the reverse of wave foiling — watch for the *next* bump behind the one you're on, since it draws water off the one in front. Rates Hood River ideal because an outgoing tide against the wind stacks the bumps.
- Klaas Voget (Duotone, Ep 73) found his first downwind winter humbling but rewarding; prefers a hardboard for weight/performance but rates Duotone's inflatable downwind board for ding-resistance and value.
- Julien Salles (F-One, Ep 47): downwinding is, for him, better than pure surfing; F-One's new Jam wing series (1900 dock-start / 1600 to-learn) is built specifically for downwind learners.
- Tiesda You (Starboard, Ep 110): the parawing is currently an accessory to downwind foiling rather than its own discipline; cites an optimised MF downwind prototype tuned to casual wing-foiling speeds (19-24 knots).
- Dave Kalama (Ep 142) has downwinded ~10 years on Maui, rides F4 Orca 685 for group crossings; advises beginners ride a longer board while learning fundamentals rather than a short 'crossover' board.
- Mike Raper (North, Ep 24) confirms North has a downwind board-and-foil programme in active development.
