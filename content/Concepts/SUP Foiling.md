# SUP Foiling

Stand-up paddle foiling — paddling to catch a bump or wave and then flying, the paddle-up skill that underpins [[Downwind]] foiling. Liam tries it for the first time in Ireland (Ep 8) and the hosts work on flat-water paddle-ups through the later episodes (Ep 108, Ep 112).

- Closely tied to [[Downwind Boards]] and the [[Downwind]] discipline; the ongoing SUP-vs-[[Parawing]] debate frames much of the 2025 catalogue (Ep 109).
- Coaching reference: [[James Casey]] (Ep 17) and [[Voyager Foiling]] (Ep 96).

## Ripple additions (batch 2, 2026-07-20)

- Ep 142 (Dave Kalama): design-first-principle is efficiency to get up on foil above all else; beginner advice is to ride a longer board while learning fundamentals.
