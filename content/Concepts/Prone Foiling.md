# Prone Foiling

Surf foiling by paddling into a wave lying prone on the board, as in surfing, then flying on the foil. The hosts openly describe it as the hardest, most humbling discipline they attempt (Ep 18, "why do we even bother?").

- Their own prone journey: early struggles (Ep 18), a breakthrough run of conditions in [[Cornwall|Newquay]] (Ep 70), and mixed sessions across [[Bantham]] and [[Exmouth]].
- Prone specialists interviewed: [[Tom Earl]], the UK's most prominent prone foil surfer (Ep 27), and [[Luke Atkinson]], a leading Australian/French prone competitor (Ep 131).
- [[Foil Assist]] (FoilDrive) is discussed as a way into the surf and for catching more waves (Ep 120).
