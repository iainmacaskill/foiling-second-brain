# S - Ep 147 Casa Del Liam and Freddies trip to Mauritius

Recorded in [[Liam]]'s garden during a UK heatwave, after ~two months of almost no foiling — Liam buried in a house renovation, [[Freddie]] away on a media trip to [[Mauritius]] and no wind at home. A wide-ranging catch-up: house DIY, a flat-water kite event, the state of parawing sales, the death of downwind paddle foiling "for the masses," and the season's first downwind race.

## In brief
Two months of "no foiling" explained: Liam's been rewiring and re-flooring his house (partner Eleanor decorating), Freddie spent a week as *media* at a marketing-driven flat-water kite event in Mauritius — and, to his own embarrassment, "hasn't ridden a single wave." Around the shenanigans, the real industry meat is Freddie's read on **parawing** booming while wing sales fall off a cliff, and both hosts geeking out over **[[Edo Tanas|Edo]] winning the season's first downwind race on F4 foils rather than his sponsor Slingshot's** — and the community's split reaction to Slingshot's openness about it.
